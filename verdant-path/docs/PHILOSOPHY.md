# 🌿 Verdant Path — Training Philosophy

> *Resilience, longevity, survivability — through embodied awareness, progressive
> oscillation, and auto-regulatory training.*

Verdant Path is the training system this app encodes. It is built around the
conviction that **creation as connection > mechanics**, and that training should
serve a long, adaptable life rather than peak numbers that fade.

---

## 🎯 Goals

| Priority | Goal | Why |
|----------|------|-----|
| Primary | **Resilience, longevity, endurance** | Diminishing returns make raw strength a poor end in itself. |
| Secondary | Strength, hypertrophy, mobility, work capacity, cardio | Support and round out the primary. |

---

## 🔄 Progressive Oscillation

Training is cyclical, not linear. Each cycle moves through:

1. **Stress** — load, tempo, or novelty introduced.
2. **Adaptation** — the body responds.
3. **Integration** — the new capacity becomes baseline.

The oscillation variables: **load, reps, sets, tempo, ROM, rest, density,
control, RPE.**

### Auto-Regulation

Train from **embodied awareness**. Use metrics (HRV, sleep, soreness) as *sanity
checks*, not dogma. The body's signal outranks the spreadsheet.

---

## 🎚 TRIMP — Training Impulse

A subjective 1–3 stress scale per session. A foundation week targets **8–12
TRIMP**.

| Level | Meaning | Examples |
|-------|---------|----------|
| 1 — Low | recovery / Zone 2 | walking, mobility |
| 2 — Medium | moderate training | normal gym session |
| 3 — High | heavy / CNS-demanding | HIIT, heavy lifts |

## 🚦 Fatigue Color Cues

Daily fatigue (from a 5-signal check-in) maps to a cue that guides the day:

| Cue | Fatigue | Guidance |
|-----|--------|----------|
| 🟢 | 0–39% | Train normally. |
| 🟠 | 40–69% | Prioritize technique, mobility, Zone 2. |
| 🔴 | 70–100% | Reduce intensity/volume; recover. |

The check-in formula normalizes five signals onto 0–100 before averaging:

```
fatigue% = 100 - mean(Energy, Mood, 100-Soreness, SleepPct, HRVPct)
```

HRV is scaled against a personal baseline so the cue adapts to the individual.

## 🏋️ The Six Fundamental Patterns

Every week — regardless of training frequency — must hit all six:

`push · pull · hinge · lunge · squat · carry/rotate/anti-rotate`

### Splits by Frequency

| Frequency | Split |
|-----------|-------|
| 1x/week | Full-Body (all patterns in one session) |
| 2x/week | Upper / Lower |
| 3x/week | Push / Pull / Legs |
| 4x/week | Back/Hams · Chest/Bi · Calves/Quads · Shoulders/Tris |
| 5x/week | Posterior Chain · Chest+Bi · Shoulders+Tri · Quads+Calves · Core+Forearms+Upper Back |
| 6x/week | One pattern per day |

### Session Structure

1. **Warm-Up** — CARs, movement prep, light cardio
2. **Main Work** — 3–5 primary movements for the day's pattern
3. **Accessory / Finisher** — unilateral, contralateral, grip, core, weak-link
4. **Conditioning** — Zone 2/LISS, threshold, intervals, rucking, natural movement
5. **Cooldown** — breathing, stretching, walking, sauna/swim

### Preferred Rotations

- **Option 1**: alternate strength and endurance days, infinitely.
- **Option 2**: 2 endurance days (1 LISS, 1 work-capacity) + 1 strength day
  (auto-regulated).

## 🌱 The 90-Day Foundational Habits Program

Habit stacking builds the lifestyle foundation under the training.

- **Morning (minimum):** 10+ min meditation · water · brush teeth · to-do list ·
  write goals
- **Daily:** 1 gallon water · 4-Day Holistic Wellness Workout Cycle (2 movement
  sessions/day) · one diet (Kauffmann/Paleo/Whole30) · no phone in bed
- **Evening (minimum):** review to-do · write goals · read 15 min · wind-down/tapping

### The 4-Day Holistic Wellness Cycle

| Day | Focus |
|-----|-------|
| 1 | Resistance — 6 fundamental movements (splittable into 2 workouts) |
| 2 | Cardio — LISS Zone 2 → Lactate Threshold → LISS → Fartlek |
| 3 | Active Recovery |
| 4 | Explosive / Ballistic / Functional / Bodyweight / Outdoor |

### Gradual Buildup

Start with 1 movement session/day (30+ min). Add a second (15–20 min Zone 2 or
mobility) as capacity improves. **End of 90 days:** daily progress photo + a
48-hour fast.

## 📊 Reviews & ACWR

Each week, compare training stress to the recent average:

**ACWR = this week's TRIMP ÷ average of prior weeks' TRIMP**

| Signal | Action |
|--------|--------|
| ACWR > 1.5 **and** HRV ↓ | Consider a deload week |
| ACWR ≈ 1.0 **and** HRV ↑ | Progressive load OK |
| 🔴 fatigue 3+ days | Consider a deload week |

## 🧭 Ethos

Metrics are sanity checks. The body's awareness has the final word. Train to be
resilient, adaptable, and present — for a long time.
