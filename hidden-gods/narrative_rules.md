## 📅 Session Structure

A typical session of **Hidden Gods** follows this structure:

### **1. Opening (15-30 minutes)**
- **Check in** with each other (how’s everyone doing? any real-life updates?).
- **Review the World Bible** (what’s been established so far?).
- **Set intentions** for the session (what do players hope to explore?).
- The **Facilitator presents the current situation** (where are we? what’s happening?).

### **2. Play (2-3 hours)**
- Characters **take actions** (roll dice, make moves).
- The **story unfolds** organically.
- **World-building happens** in real-time (new layers, anomalies, or lore may emerge).
- **Anomalies are discovered and explored**.

### **3. Closing (30-60 minutes)**
- **Wrap up loose ends** (resolve cliffhangers or save them for next time).
- **Discuss what happened** (what were the key moments? how did the story evolve?).
- **Record the session** (see below).
- **Plan for next time** (what’s the cliffhanger? what do we want to explore next?).

## 🎙️ Recording Sessions

To transform your play into **stories and episodes**, recording is **essential**. Below are options for capturing your sessions:

### **Option 1: Audio Recording**
- **Tools**: Use a phone, tablet, or dedicated recorder.
- **Tips**:
  - Ensure **everyone consents** to being recorded.
  - Test the **audio quality** beforehand.
  - Start recording **at the beginning of play** (including the Opening).
  - Pause during breaks if needed.
  - Announce when **starting/stopping** the recording.
- **File Naming**: Save files with **date and session number** (e.g., `HiddenGods_Session01_2026-08-01.mp3`).

### **Option 2: Note-Taking**
- **Tools**: Use a notebook, shared digital doc (Google Docs, Notion), or a note-taking app.
- **Tips**:
  - Have a **dedicated scribe** or rotate the role.
  - Capture **key dialogue** (not every word, but the important exchanges).
  - Note **important events and decisions** (e.g., "The party discovered the Echoing Door").
  - Record **world-building elements** (e.g., new anomalies, lore, or layer rules).

### **Option 3: Video Recording**
- **Tools**: Use a camera, phone, or screen recording software (e.g., OBS, Zoom).
- **Tips**:
  - Requires **more setup** (lighting, camera angles, etc.).
  - Can be **edited into episodes** later.
  - Useful for **visual storytelling** (e.g., maps, character art).

## 📝 Transcribing to Stories

After the session, **transcribe the recording** into a **narrative format**. This is where the magic of Hidden Gods happens: **play becomes story**.

### **Transcription Process**
1. **Listen/Review**: Go through the recording and notes.
2. **Identify Key Moments**: Highlight:
   - Important **decisions and actions**.
   - **Dialogue** that reveals character or advances the plot.
   - **World-building revelations** (new lore, anomalies, or layers).
   - **Character development** (internal conflicts, growth, or setbacks).
   - **Emotional or dramatic peaks** (climaxes, revelations, or turning points).
3. **Write the Narrative**: Transform the play into a **story**.
   - Use **third-person limited or omniscient perspective**.
   - Include **internal monologues** (from character parts).
   - Describe the **world as it’s revealed**.
   - Add **sensory details** (sights, sounds, smells).
4. **Edit**: Refine the story for **clarity and flow**.
5. **Share**: Distribute to the group for **feedback**.

### **Example: From Session to Story**
**Session Excerpt**:
> *Player 1 (The Creator): "I want to carve a symbol into the stone. I roll+Weird... got a 9."*
> *Facilitator: "Okay, you carve the symbol, but as you do, you feel a presence watching you. What do you do?"*
> *Player 1: "I turn to face it. I roll+Sharp to read the situation... got a 12."*
> *Facilitator: "You see a figure made of shifting shadows. It gestures toward the symbol, then toward the horizon."*

**Story Version**:
> The Creator’s chisel bit into the stone with each deliberate strike. The symbol was taking shape—a spiral within a circle, the mark of the Hidden Gods. As the final line was carved, a chill ran down their spine. They were not alone.
>
> Turning, they saw a figure composed of darkness and light, shifting like smoke in the wind. It raised a hand—if it was a hand—and pointed first to the newly carved symbol, then to the distant horizon where the sun bled into the earth.
>
> The Creator’s breath caught. The symbol pulsed with a faint inner light, and for a moment, they understood: the figure was not a threat, but an invitation. The Hidden Gods were watching. And they were pleased.

## 📖 Episode Creation

For a **more serialized format**, combine **multiple sessions** into **episodes** of your saga.

### **Episode Structure**
An episode might contain:
- **Prologue**: Setting the stage (where are we? what’s the mood?).
- **Act 1: Introduction of New Elements** (new characters, anomalies, or layers).
- **Act 2: Development and Conflict** (characters grapple with challenges).
- **Act 3: Climax and Resolution** (the big reveal or turning point).
- **Epilogue**: Reflection and foreshadowing (what did we learn? what’s next?).

### **Episode Arcs**
| Arc Type | Description | Example |
|----------|-------------|---------|
| **Character Arcs** | How a character changes over several episodes. | The Creator learns to trust their intuition. |
| **World Arcs** | How the world evolves. | The Debug Layer begins to collapse. |
| **Mystery Arcs** | Unfolding anomalies and questions. | The party uncovers the truth behind the Whispering Stones. |

## 🌌 The Hidden Gods Saga

The collection of **all your stories and episodes** forms the **Hidden Gods Saga**—an **ongoing, evolving narrative** that grows with each session.

### **Saga Elements**
| Element | Description | Example |
|---------|-------------|---------|
| **The World Bible** | The collaborative world document. | A Notion page with layers, gods, and anomalies. |
| **Character Journals** | Individual records of each character’s journey. | A notebook for each player’s alter ego. |
| **Episode Archive** | All the stories and episodes created. | A folder of Markdown files or a blog. |
| **Anomaly Log** | A record of all anomalies and their status. | A table tracking resolved/unresolved anomalies. |

### **Example Saga Structure**
```
Hidden Gods Saga/
├── World Bible/
│   ├── premise.md
│   ├── setting.md
│   ├── hidden_gods.md
│   └── anomalies.md
├── Episodes/
│   ├── Episode 01: The Echoing Door.md
│   ├── Episode 02: The Whispering Stones.md
│   └── Episode 03: The Navigator’s Gambit.md
├── Character Journals/
│   ├── The Creator.md
│   ├── The Sage.md
│   └── The Rebel.md
└── Anomaly Log.md
```
