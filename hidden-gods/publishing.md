## The Editing Process

Transforming a session recording into an episode involves:

1. **Listen/Review**: Go through the recording and notes.
2. **Identify Key Moments**: Highlight important scenes, dialogue, and decisions.
3. **Write the Narrative**: Transform the play into a story.
   - Use third-person limited or omniscient perspective.
   - Include internal monologues (from character parts).
   - Describe the world as it is revealed.
4. **Edit**: Refine the story for clarity and flow.
5. **Share**: Distribute to the group for feedback.

---

## Story Structure

Each session can become one or more short stories or episodes. Structure them with:

- A **title** that captures the essence.
- An **opening** that sets the scene.
- **Scenes** that follow the action.
- **Internal reflections** from characters.
- A **closing** that hints at what is next.

## Example: From Session to Story

**Session Excerpt**:

Player 1 (The Creator): "I want to carve a symbol into the stone. I roll+Weird... got a 9."

Facilitator: "Okay, you carve the symbol, but as you do, you feel a presence watching you. What do you do?"

Player 1: "I turn to face it. I roll+Sharp to read the situation... got a 12."

Facilitator: "You see a figure made of shifting shadows. It gestures toward the symbol, then toward the horizon."

**Story Version**:

The Creator’s chisel bit into the stone with each deliberate strike. The symbol was taking shape—a spiral within a circle, the mark of the Hidden Gods. As the final line was carved, a chill ran down their spine. They were not alone.

Turning, they saw a figure composed of darkness and light, shifting like smoke in the wind. It raised a hand—if it was a hand—and pointed first to the newly carved symbol, then to the distant horizon where the sun bled into the earth.

The Creator’s breath caught. The symbol pulsed with a faint inner light, and for a moment, they understood: the figure was not a threat, but an invitation. The Hidden Gods were watching. And they were pleased.

## Formats for Sharing

Your Hidden Gods saga can take many forms:

- **Digital Archive**: A website or blog where episodes are posted.
- **Printed Zine**: A physical collection of stories.
- **Audio Drama**: Turn sessions into a podcast.
- **Social Media**: Share snippets and updates.

---

## Building a Community

As your saga grows, you might want to share it with others:

- Invite others to play: Expand your group and introduce new perspectives.
- Host reading events: Share your stories with a wider audience.
- Collaborate with other groups: Connect with other Hidden Gods players to create a shared multiverse.
- Create a shared wiki: Document your world for others to explore.

---

## The Living Saga

The beauty of Hidden Gods is that the saga is never finished. Each new session adds to the world, deepens the characters, and expands the narrative. Over time, your collection of stories becomes a rich, complex tapestry of collaborative creation.
