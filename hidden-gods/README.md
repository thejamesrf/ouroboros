---

## 🌌 **What is Hidden Gods?**
Hidden Gods is a **PbtA tabletop RPG** where players explore a **nested simulation**—a reality composed of layers, each with its own rules, gods, and secrets. The game blends:
- **Simulation Hypothesis**: Reality is a stack of simulations, and the Hidden Gods are the architects, admins, or players of higher layers.
- **Jungian Archetypes & IFS**: Characters are shaped by inner parts (e.g., the Shadow, the Sage) that reflect their role in the simulation.
- **Collaborative World-Building**: Players and Facilitators co-create the layers, anomalies, and lore.

> *"You are not just playing a game. You are playing with the code of reality itself."*

---

## 🎲 **Core Mechanics**
### **Stats (PbtA Style)**
Players roll **2d6 + Stat** (e.g., `Roll+Weird` to hack the simulation). Core stats:
- **Weird**: Your connection to the simulation’s glitches and hidden layers.
- **Cool**: Your ability to stay grounded and navigate chaos.
- **Sharp**: Your insight into the simulation’s rules and patterns.
- **Hot**: Your passion and drive to change or break the system.
- **Charm**: Your ability to influence others (NPCs or players) within the simulation.

### **Moves**
Moves are actions players can take to interact with the simulation. Examples:
- **Hack the Code**: *Roll+Weird* to manipulate the simulation’s rules.
- **Layer Hop**: *Roll+Cool* to move between simulation layers.
- **Glitch Out**: *Roll+Hot* to force an anomaly to manifest.
- **Introspect**: *Roll+Sharp* to uncover a hidden part of your character.
- **Negotiate with a God**: *Roll+Charm* to bargain with a Hidden God.

### **Anomalies**
Anomalies are **glitches, clues, or disruptions** in the simulation. They can:
- Reveal higher/lower layers.
- Test the players’ understanding of the simulation.
- Offer risks or rewards (e.g., `2-Weird` to resist disorientation).

Example:
```markdown
🔍 **Anomaly: The Echoing Door**
- **Manifestation**: A door that repeats the last 3 seconds of sound when opened.
- **Clue**: "The air smells like ozone."
- **Purpose**: To test the party’s perception of time.
- **Risk**: Roll+Weird to resist disorientation (2-Weird).
```

## 🗂️ **Simulation Layers**
Layers are **nested levels of reality** in the game. Each has its own:
- **Theme**: Aesthetic and mood (e.g., "Glitchy, monochrome, and full of floating symbols").
- **Rules**: How physics, time, and logic work (or don’t work).
- **Gods**: Hidden Gods who administer or play in that layer.
- **Anomalies**: Unique glitches or clues.

### **Example Layers**
| Layer          | Theme                          | Rules                                                                 | Gods               |
|----------------|--------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------|--------------------|
| **Base Reality** | "Normal" life                 | Standard physics, but with subtle glitches.                          | The Architect     |
| **Debug**       | Glitchy, monochrome           | Code is visible as geometry; time is non-linear.                     | The Debugger      |
| **Dream**       | Surreal, emotional             | Rules are fluid; emotions shape reality.                            | The Dreamer       |
| **Machine**     | Mechanical, cold              | Everything is a machine; free will is an illusion.                   | The Engineer      |

## 🛠️ **Getting Started**
### **For Players**
1. Read the [`simulation_bible.md`](simulation_bible.md) to understand the setting.
2. Choose or create a **playbook** (archetype) from [`/playbooks`](playbooks/).
3. Work with your Facilitator to define the **starting layer** and **anomalies**.

### **For Facilitators**
1. Review the **core moves** and **layer rules** in [`/moves`](moves/) and [`simulation_bible.md`](simulation_bible.md).
2. Prepare **anomalies** and **gods** for your session (or generate them using tools from [`/src`](../src)).
3. Encourage players to **collaborate on world-building** (e.g., "What does the Debug layer look like to you?").
