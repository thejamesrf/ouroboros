# Final Notes

Hidden Gods is a game about **collaboration, creation, and connection**. The rules are a framework, but the real magic happens in the space between the players as they build a world together.

---

## Core Philosophy

- **Creation as Connection**: Building a world together is the primary activity.
- **The Blurred Line**: The boundary between storyteller and story is intentionally porous.
- **Mythic Resonance**: Every session contributes to an evolving saga.
- **Personal Discovery**: Your alter ego is both a fictional character and a vehicle for self-exploration.

---

## Remember

- There are **no wrong answers** in world-building.
- **Every voice matters** in the narrative.
- The saga **belongs to everyone** who contributes to it.
- The **Hidden Gods are watching**—and they are pleased with what you create.
