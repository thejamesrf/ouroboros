# Adapted Apocalypse World Moves

---

## Battle Moves

### Seize by Force
When you use overwhelming force to take something by violence, **roll+Hard**.

- **10+**: You take it. Choose one:
  - They have no chance to fight back.
  - You do not take significant harm.
  - You take something extra of value.
- **7-9**: You take it, but choose one:
  - You take harm.
  - They get a chance to fight back or escape.
  - You leave something of value behind.
- **Miss**: You do not take it.

---

### Single Combat
When you engage in one-on-one combat, **roll+Hard**.

- **10+**: You dominate your opponent. Choose one:
  - You force them to retreat.
  - You disarm or incapacitate them.
  - You take something from them.
- **7-9**: You hold your own, but choose one:
  - You take harm.
  - They get a chance to strike back.
  - You lose your footing or advantage.

---

## Road War Moves

### Board a Moving Vehicle
When you try to get onto a moving vehicle, **roll+Cool**.

- **10+**: You make it aboard safely. Choose one:
  - You bring someone or something with you.
  - You secure a favorable position.
  - You do it without drawing attention.
- **7-9**: You make it, but barely. Choose one:
  - You are exposed or vulnerable.
  - You leave someone or something behind.
  - You draw unwanted attention.

---

### Outdistance Another Vehicle
When you try to escape or outrun another vehicle, **roll+Cool**.

- **10+**: You lose them. Choose one:
  - You do it without damage or delay.
  - You know where they are and can double back.
  - You create an obstacle for them.
- **7-9**: You get some distance, but choose one:
  - They are still on your tail.
  - You take damage or a penalty.
  - You lose something in the process.
