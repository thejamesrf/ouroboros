# Sample Anomalies

> 🔍 *Ready-to-drop anomalies for Facilitators.*

## 🔍 **The Echoing Door**
- **Manifestation**: A door that repeats the last 3 seconds of sound when opened.
- **Clue**: "The air smells like ozone."
- **Purpose**: To test the party's perception of time.
- **Risk**: Roll+Weird to resist disorientation (2-Weird).

## 🪞 **The Mirror That Remembers**
- **Manifestation**: A mirror that shows the reflection of whoever last looked into it—even if that was hours ago.
- **Clue**: "Your reflection blinks a half-second too late."
- **Purpose**: To hint that the Base Reality layer is caching, not generating.
- **Risk**: Roll+Sharp or lose track of which reflection is real.

## 🕳️ **The Stair That Loops**
- **Manifestation**: A staircase that returns to its starting step after exactly 13 steps.
- **Clue**: "The step numbers reset, but the dust doesn't."
- **Purpose**: A seam between layers—climbing it can trigger a Layer Hop.
- **Risk**: Roll+Cool or be deposited in an unintended layer.
