# Sample Session: "The First Cycle"
> <span style="background-color: #4CAF50; color: white; padding: 2px 6px; border-radius: 4px;">🟢 World-Building</span>
> <span style="background-color: #9C27B0; color: white; padding: 2px 6px; border-radius: 4px;">🟣 Introspection</span>
> *A complete example of a Hidden Gods session, from character creation to the first cycle completion.*

---

## 🎭 **Session Overview**
**Facilitator**: James
**Players**:
- **Alice** (The Hacker)
- **Bob** (The Glitch)
- **Charlie** (The Architect)

**Layers Visited**: Dream → Base Reality → Debug → **Cycle Complete**
**Session Length**: ~2 hours
**Themes**: Discovery, cyclical reality, retaining self

---

## 📜 **Part 1: Character Creation**
*James explains the setting and themes of Hidden Gods.*

**James**: "You’re all part of a **nested simulation**—a reality composed of layers, each with its own rules and gods. Your characters are **archetypes** shaped by their roles in the simulation. Today, you’ll explore these layers, encounter anomalies, and maybe even **break the cycle**."

*Players choose their playbooks:*
- **Alice** picks **The Hacker** ("I want to rewrite reality!").
- **Bob** picks **The Glitch** ("I want to break everything!").
- **Charlie** picks **The Architect** ("I want to understand the design!").

*James asks each player to describe their character’s backstory in one sentence:*
- **Alice**: "I once rewrote the rules of a simulation layer to save a friend. The system still bears my fingerprints."
- **Bob**: "I don’t remember a time when I wasn’t breaking things. The simulation fears me."
- **Charlie**: "I designed a layer so perfect that even I couldn’t escape it. Then I did."

---

## 🌌 **Part 2: The Dream Layer**
*James describes the opening scene.*

**James**: "You find yourselves standing in a **dimly lit library**. The air smells of old paper and something… *electric*. The bookshelves stretch impossibly high, and the books seem to **rearrange themselves** when you’re not looking. A **clock on the wall** ticks backward. What do you do?"

### **Alice’s Turn (The Hacker)**
**Alice**: "I use **Code Sense** to see if there’s hidden code in the library."
- Rolls **2d6 + Weird (2)** → **8 (Success!)**
- **James**: "You see **flickering lines of code** beneath the surface of reality. The library is a **simulation construct**, and the code is **old—ancient, even**. You notice a **backdoor** hidden in the clock’s mechanism. It leads to… somewhere else."

**Alice**: "I use **Hack the Code** to open the backdoor."
- Rolls **2d6 + Weird (2)** → **10 (Success!)**
- **James**: "The clock **unfolds** like a flower, revealing a **floating door** covered in glowing symbols. It hums with energy. The door is now open."

### **Bob’s Turn (The Glitch)**
**Bob**: "I use **Glitch Out** to make the door even weirder."
- Rolls **2d6 + Hot (2)** → **7 (Success!)**
- **James**: "The door **distorts**, its edges bleeding into the air like ink in water. The symbols **rearrange** into a message: *‘The first step is the last.’*"

### **Charlie’s Turn (The Architect)**
**Charlie**: "I use **Blueprint Vision** to understand the door’s structure."
- Rolls **2d6 + Sharp (2)** → **9 (Success!)**
- **James**: "You see the **underlying architecture** of the door. It’s a **gateway to the Base Reality Layer**, but it’s **unstable**. If you step through, it might collapse—or worse, **take you somewhere unexpected**."

### **Group Decision**
The players decide to **step through the door**.

---

## 🏙️ **Part 3: Base Reality**
*James describes the transition.*

**James**: "You step through the door—and suddenly, you’re standing on a **city street**. The air is crisp, the sky is blue, and everything looks… *normal*. But then you notice the **cracks**: a **billboard flickers** with the message *‘WAKE UP’*, a **stranger hands you a note** in your own handwriting, and a **building has a floor that doesn’t exist** in the blueprints. What do you do?"

### **Alice’s Turn**
**Alice**: "I read the note."
- **James**: "The note says: *‘You are not who you think you are.’* It’s written in your handwriting, but you don’t remember writing it."
- **Alice adds the clue to her inventory.**

**Alice**: "I use **Hack the Code** to make the billboard show a different message."
- Rolls **2d6 + Weird (2)** → **5 (Partial Success)**
- **James**: "The billboard **flickers** and changes to read: *‘THE CODE IS THE TRUTH.’* But then it **short-circuits** and goes dark. The stranger who gave you the note **vanishes**."

### **Bob’s Turn**
**Bob**: "I use **Glitch Out** to make the missing floor appear."
- Rolls **2d6 + Hot (2)** → **11 (Success!)**
- **James**: "The building **shudders**, and the missing floor **materializes** in front of you. The elevator doors open, revealing a button labeled *‘DEBUG.’*"

### **Charlie’s Turn**
**Charlie**: "I use **Structural Integrity** to stabilize the building."
- Rolls **2d6 + Cool (1)** → **6 (Success!)**
- **James**: "The building **stops shaking**, and the missing floor **stays in place**. The elevator hums, waiting for input."

### **Group Decision**
The players **press the DEBUG button**.

---

## 💻 **Part 4: The Debug Layer**
*James describes the transition.*

**James**: "The elevator **plummets**—not down, but *sideways*, as if reality itself is **folding**. When the doors open, you’re in a **glitchy, monochrome world**. The ground is a **grid of hexagons**, and **floating symbols** drift through the air like snow. A **terminal window** hovers nearby, displaying errors:
```
ERROR: REALITY NOT FOUND
SEGMENTATION FAULT: CORE DUMPED
```
What do you do?"

### **Alice’s Turn**
**Alice**: "I use **Rewrite Reality** to make the terminal display something useful."
- Pays **1-Weird** and rolls **2d6 + Sharp (1)** → **7 (Success!)**
- **James**: "The terminal **clears** and displays a new message:
```
WARNING: UNAUTHORIZED ACCESS
LAYER: DEBUG
USER: UNKNOWN
```
You now have **temporary admin access** to this section of the layer."

### **Bob’s Turn**
**Bob**: "I use **Static Discharge** to overload the terminal."
- Rolls **2d6 + Weird (0)** → **4 (Partial Success)**
- **James**: "The terminal **sparks**, and the symbols **scatter** like leaves in the wind. But then it **reboots** and displays a new error:
```
ERROR: USER NOT RECOGNIZED
PLEASE REBOOT
```
You take **1-Weird** from the feedback."

### **Charlie’s Turn**
**Charlie**: "I use **Admin Override** to take control of the terminal."
- Pays **1-Sharp** and rolls **2d6 + Weird (1)** → **8 (Success!)**
- **James**: "The terminal **obeys**. A new prompt appears:
```
DEBUG> _
```
You can now **input commands**."

**Charlie**: "I type ‘LAYER LIST’."
- **James**: "The terminal displays:
```
1. DREAM
2. BASE REALITY
3. DEBUG
4. ??? (LOCKED)
```
There’s a **fourth layer**—but it’s locked."

### **Group Decision**
The players decide to **return to the Dream Layer** to look for more clues.

---

## 🔄 **Part 5: The Cycle Completes**
*James describes the return to the Dream Layer.*

**James**: "You step through a **floating door** and find yourselves back in the **library**. But something is… *different*. The clock is now ticking **forward**, and the books are **arranged alphabetically**. Then you see it: a **snake eating its own tail**, floating in the air. It’s the **Ouroboros**."

### **Alice’s Turn**
**Alice**: "I use **Introspect** to understand the Ouroboros."
- Rolls **2d6 + Sharp (1)** → **9 (Success!)**
- **James**: "You realize: *‘The cycle is eternal. You are both the eater and the eaten.’* This is the **final clue**—the key to understanding the simulation."

### **Win Condition Triggered!**
**James**: "As the words sink in, the **library dissolves** around you. The Hidden Gods whisper: *‘The ouroboros eats its own tail.’* You’ve completed the **first cycle**."

**Alice**: "What happens now?"
**James**: "You **retain a piece of yourselves** from this cycle:
- **+1 to your highest stat** (Alice’s Weird increases to 3).
- **A memory**: *‘Cycle 1: The cycle is eternal. You are both the eater and the eaten.’*

A **new cycle begins**. You find yourselves back in the **Dream Layer**—but this time, the clock is ticking **backward again**."

---

## 📝 **Session Notes**
### **What Went Well**
- The players **collaborated** to solve puzzles (e.g., Alice hacked the terminal, Bob disrupted it, Charlie stabilized it).
- The **cyclical nature** of the game created a **satisfying narrative arc**.
- The **win condition** felt **earned** and **thematic**.

### **What Could Be Improved**
- **More clues** about the Ouroboros could have been dropped earlier.
- **Bob’s Glitch** could have had more **unique interactions** with the simulation.
- A **map or diagram** of the layers might have helped the players visualize the cycle.

### **For Next Session**
- Explore the **fourth layer** (???).
- **Negotiate with a Hidden God** (e.g., The Debugger).
- **Uncover more memories** from previous cycles.

## 🎯 **Facilitator Reflection**
**James**: "This session worked well because:
1. **Player Agency**: The players drove the story with their moves and choices.
2. **Thematic Depth**: The cyclical win condition tied into the **Ouroboros theme** perfectly.
3. **Collaboration**: Each playbook had a **unique role** in overcoming obstacles.

For next time, I’ll:
- **Foreshadow the Ouroboros** earlier in the session.
- **Give Bob’s Glitch** more opportunities to **disrupt reality** in creative ways.
- **Introduce a Hidden God** for a **negotiation scene**."

## 🛠️ **Facilitator Tips for Running This Session**
1. **Encourage Exploration**: Let players **describe their surroundings** in each layer.
2. **Use Anomalies as Clues**: Drop hints about the **Ouroboros** and **cyclical nature** early.
3. **Reward Creativity**: If a player describes a **clever use of a move**, give them a bonus.
4. **Embrace the Cycle**: The win condition should feel **inevitable but satisfying**—like solving a puzzle.
