# Sample Session

> 🎲 *An example Hidden Gods session, for Facilitators and players learning the game.*

This is a **brief illustrative session** showing how the core moves—`Hack the Code`, `Layer Hop`, `Introspect`, and `Negotiate with a God`—fit together at the table.

---

## **Setup**
- **Layer**: Debug (code visible as geometry; time non-linear).
- **God**: The Debugger.
- **Cast**: Alice (The Hacker), Marcus (The Glitch).

## **Scene 1: The Echoing Door**
The party finds a door that replays the last 3 seconds of sound. Alice tries to `Hack the Code` (Roll+Weird) to silence it. She rolls **11**—the door goes quiet, but the Debugger notices the edit.

## **Scene 2: Layer Hop**
Marcus wants to retreat to the Dream Layer. He `Layer Hops` (Roll+Cool) and rolls **8**—he arrives, but something followed him back: a fragment of the Echoing Door now echoes in the Dream Layer too.

## **Scene 3: Introspect**
In the Dream Layer, Alice `Introspects` (Roll+Sharp) to understand why the door unsettles her. She rolls **7**—she recalls that the sound it repeats is her own voice, from a layer she doesn't remember being in. Unsettling but true.

## **Scene 4: Negotiate with a God**
The Debugger appears. Alice `Negotiates with a God` (Roll+Charm) to undo the Door's effect. She rolls **9**—the Debugger agrees, but demands she leave one memory behind as payment.
