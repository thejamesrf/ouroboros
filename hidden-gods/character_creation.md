## 🎭 Step 1: Choose Your Archetype

Your character is built from an archetype that serves as their core identity. Choose one that resonates with you. This will be your dominant archetype.

### Major Turning Points
Each person experiences 3 to 4 pivotal moments during childhood—events that were emotionally significant, where a decision was made that "I must be" a certain way. These are experiences often involving pain, fear, or a sense of threat. These moments could be:
- A break in belonging (e.g., feeling abandoned, excluded, or rejected)
- A failure or humiliation (e.g., public embarrassment, not meeting expectations)
- A loss or betrayal (e.g., death of a loved one, a friend’s betrayal)
- A survival challenge (e.g., illness, a move, a family crisis)

### The Fragments
In response to these events, the individual unconsciously adopts strategies or traits—their alter egos—to manage the unpleasant situation. These are automatic, often positive-seeming coping mechanisms that shape personality.

| Fragment | Triggering Event | Need to Be Seen As |
|----------|-------------------|---------------------|
| Smart/Intelligent | Not having the knowledge needed | The smart one |
| Independence/Strength | Feeling abandoned | The strong, independent one |
| Kindness/Empathy | Dealing with hard-heartedness | The kind one |
| Popular/Liked by Everyone | Social rejection | The likable one |
| Perfectionism | Criticism or failure | The perfect one |
| People-Pleasing/Humor | Conflict or disapproval | The funny one |
| Emotional Numbness | Overwhelming emotion | The stoic one |

## 🏆 Core Archetypes

Choose a primary archetype that aligns with your core personality or fragment. Then, choose a secondary archetype to temper and complement your primary.

| Archetype | Core Drive | Shadow Aspect |
|-----------|------------|----------------|
| The Innocent | Seeks safety, purity | Naivety, denial |
| The Orphan | Belongs, fits in | Cynicism, conformity |
| The Hero | Overcomes adversity | Arrogance, violence |
| The Caregiver | Nurtures, protects | Martyrdom, control |
| The Explorer | Discovers, escapes | Restlessness, aimlessness |
| The Rebel | Challenges, disrupts | Destructiveness, alienation |
| The Lover | Connects, experiences | Addiction, obsession |
| The Creator | Imagines, innovates | Perfectionism, detachment |
| The Jester | Enjoys, plays | Irresponsibility, cruelty |
| The Sage | Understands, seeks truth | Dogmatism, inaction |
| The Magician | Transforms, manifests | Manipulation, deception |
| The Ruler | Controls, orders | Tyranny, rigidity |

### Secondary Archetype Benefits
Your secondary archetype provides:
- One additional move from its move list
- A stat adjustment (+1 or -1 to one stat)
- Synergy or conflict with your primary archetype

## 🧠 Step 2: Define Your IFS Parts

Internal Family Systems recognizes that we all have different parts within us. Choose 2-3 parts that influence your character.

| Part | Role | Perspective | Relationship with Other Parts |
|------|------|-------------|-------------------------------|
| The Manager | Organized, controlling | I must plan for everything. | Often clashes with the Firefighter. |
| The Exile | Wounded, young | I carry the pain of the past. | Protected by the Manager. |
| The Firefighter | Impulsive, reactive | I must distract from the pain. | Disrupts the Manager’s plans. |
| The Critic | Judgmental, perfectionistic | Nothing is ever good enough. | Targets the Exile and the Creator. |

## 🎭 Step 3: Create Your Alter Ego

Your alter ego is your character in the world of Hidden Gods. They are:
- Shaped by your archetype and parts
- An expression of your inner landscape
- A vessel for exploration and growth

Name your alter ego—something evocative and meaningful to you.

## 📊 Step 4: Assign Stats

| Stat | Represents | Example Actions |
|------|------------|-----------------|
| Cool | Grace under pressure, composure | Acting under fire, staying calm in chaos |
| Hard | Physical power, endurance | Going aggro, withstanding harm |
| Hot | Charisma, passion, volatility | Seduction, manipulation, emotional intensity |
| Sharp | Perception, insight, intelligence | Reading a situation, reading a person |
| Weird | Connection to the psychic maelstrom, intuition | Opening your brain, psychic phenomena |

### Stat Arrays
Choose one of the following stat arrays (or create your own with group approval):

| Array | Cool | Hard | Hot | Sharp | Weird |
|-------|------|------|-----|-------|------|
| Balanced | +1 | +1 | -1 | +1 | =0 |
| Perceptive | +2 | =0 | +1 | -1 | +1 |
| Resilient | =0 | +2 | +1 | =0 | -1 |
| Charismatic | +1 | -1 | +2 | =0 | +1 |

## 🎯 Step 5: Choose Moves

All characters get the basic moves. Then, choose 2 special moves from your archetype’s list or the general list.

### Example Archetype Moves

#### The Creator
- **World-Shaper**: When you describe a new aspect of the world, it becomes real. Roll+Weird. On a 10+, it manifests perfectly. On a 7-9, it manifests but with a twist. On a miss, it manifests in a dangerous or unexpected way.
- **Artistic Vision**: When you create something (art, music, a story), roll+Sharp. On a hit, it resonates with the psychic maelstrom and affects those who experience it.

#### The Sage
- **Deep Insight**: When you seek understanding of a person or situation, roll+Sharp. On a 10+, ask 3 questions. On a 7-9, ask 1.
- **Lorekeeper**: Once per session, you can recall or invent a relevant piece of lore about the world.

#### The Rebel
- **Defy Authority**: When you challenge a rule or norm, roll+Hot. On a hit, you inspire others to question as well.
- **Unbreakable**: When you suffer harm for standing your ground, take +1 armor.

#### The Explorer
- **Pathfinder**: When you navigate unfamiliar terrain, roll+Sharp. On a 10+, you find the safest route and avoid dangers. On a 7-9, you find a route but face a hazard.
- **Curiosity’s Reward**: When you investigate an anomaly, roll+Weird. On a hit, you uncover a hidden truth or secret.

## 🤝 Step 6: Define Relationships (Hx)

At the start of the first session, go around and ask each other character one question from your archetype’s list. Based on their answer, assign Hx (History) values:

| Hx Value | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| +3 | Deep connection, shared history, strong bond |
| +2 | Friendly, respectful, some history |
| +1 | Neutral, passing acquaintance |
| 0 | Indifferent, no history |
| -1 | Distrustful, tense |
| -2 | Hostile, past conflict |

### Example Hx Questions
- Which of you would I trust with my life?
- Which of you challenges me the most?
- Which of you do I feel most protective of?
- Which of you has seen me at my worst?

### Highlighting Stats
At the end of character creation:
1. Each player chooses one character with the highest Hx and asks that player to highlight one of their stats.
2. The Facilitator highlights a second stat for each character.
