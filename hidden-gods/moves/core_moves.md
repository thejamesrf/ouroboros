# Core Moves

> 🎲 *The base moves available to every character in Hidden Gods.*

Moves are actions players take to interact with the simulation. On a move, **roll 2d6 + Stat**:

- **10+**: Full success—you do it.
- **7–9**: Partial success—you do it, but with a cost or complication.
- **6-**: The Facilitator makes a hard move; things get worse.

---

## **Hack the Code** *(Roll+Weird)*
Manipulate the simulation's rules by rewriting its Ontos statements.
- **10+**: The change takes hold cleanly.
- **7–9**: It works, but the simulation pushes back—an anomaly manifests or a god notices.
- **6-**: You break something you didn't mean to.

## **Layer Hop** *(Roll+Cool)*
Move between simulation layers (e.g., Debug → Dream).
- **10+**: You arrive exactly where you intended.
- **7–9**: You arrive, but off-target or with something following you.
- **6-**: You end up in the wrong layer entirely.

## **Glitch Out** *(Roll+Hot)*
Force an anomaly to manifest through sheer will.
- **10+**: The anomaly obeys you.
- **7–9**: It manifests, but not the way you wanted.
- **6-**: The glitch turns on you.

## **Introspect** *(Roll+Sharp)*
Uncover a hidden part of your character (an IFS part or Jungian archetype).
- **10+**: Gain clear insight and a new option.
- **7–9**: You learn something true, but it's unsettling.
- **6-**: A part takes over.

## **Negotiate with a God** *(Roll+Charm)*
Bargain with a Hidden God.
- **10+**: They grant your request on your terms.
- **7–9**: They agree, but the price is steep.
- **6-**: They refuse—or worse, accept on *their* terms.
