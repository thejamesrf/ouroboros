> *The fundamental actions players can take in Hidden Gods.*

---

## 🎲 **How Moves Work**
In Hidden Gods, players **roll 2d6 + a stat** to perform moves. The outcome determines success, partial success, or failure:

| **Result**       | **Outcome**                                                                                     |
|------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **6+**           | **Success**: The move works as intended.                                                        |
| **4-5**          | **Partial Success**: The move works, but with a cost or complication.                          |
| **2-3**          | **Failure**: The move fails, and something bad happens (or a Hidden God appears).          |

---

## 📜 **Core Moves**
These moves are **available to all playbooks** unless noted otherwise.

### **1. Hack the Code** *(Roll+Weird)*
*Manipulate the simulation’s rules to achieve an effect.*

**Example Uses**:
- Open a locked door in the Debug Layer.
- Rewrite a section of reality to remove an obstacle.
- Force an anomaly to manifest.

**Success (6+)**:
The code bends to your will. The effect you desired happens.

**Partial Success (4-5)**:
The code resists. The effect happens, but:
- You take **1-Weird** (disorientation from the effort).
- The simulation **notices you** (a Hidden God may appear).

**Failure (2-3)**:
The code backfires. Roll on the **Glitch Table** (see below).

---

### **2. Layer Hop** *(Roll+Cool)*
*Attempt to transition to another simulation layer.*

**Example Uses**:
- Step through a portal to the Base Reality Layer.
- Escape the Debug Layer before it collapses.
- Follow a clue to a higher layer.

**Success (6+)**:
You transition smoothly to the **next layer in the cycle** (Dream → Base Reality → Debug → Dream).

**Partial Success (4-5)**:
You transition, but to a **random layer** (not necessarily the next one).

**Failure (2-3)**:
You fail to transition and **remain in your current layer**. Additionally:
- You take **1-Cool** (the effort leaves you shaken).
- The layer **reacts negatively** (e.g., anomalies become more hostile).

---

### **3. Introspect** *(Roll+Sharp)*
*Uncover a hidden part of your character or understand a clue.*

**Example Uses**:
- Decipher the meaning of an anomaly’s clue.
- Recall a forgotten memory from a previous cycle.
- Discover a new aspect of your playbook’s backstory.

**Success (6+)**:
You gain **deep insight**. Choose one:
- Learn the **true nature** of an anomaly or layer.
- Uncover a **hidden memory** (add a clue to your inventory).
- Gain **+1 Sharp** (temporary or permanent, Facilitator’s choice).

**Partial Success (4-5)**:
You learn **something**, but it’s incomplete or misleading.

**Failure (2-3)**:
You **misinterpret** the clue or memory, leading to:
- A **false belief** (Facilitator may feed you incorrect information).
- **1-Sharp** (your mind is clouded).

---

### **4. Glitch Out** *(Roll+Hot)*
*Force an anomaly to manifest or break the simulation’s rules.*

**Example Uses**:
- Summon a glitch to distract a Hidden God.
- Overload a section of the Debug Layer to create a path.
- Cause a **controlled chaos** to escape a trap.

**Success (6+)**:
The glitch manifests as you intended. The simulation **reacts violently** but in your favor.

**Partial Success (4-5)**:
The glitch manifests, but:
- It’s **unpredictable** (Facilitator chooses the effect).
- You take **1-Hot** (the effort exhausts you).

**Failure (2-3)**:
The glitch **backfires**. Roll on the **Glitch Table**.

---

### **5. Negotiate with a God** *(Roll+Charm)*
*Bargain, persuade, or deceive a Hidden God.*

**Example Uses**:
- Convince The Architect to reveal a secret.
- Strike a deal with The Dreamer for safe passage.
- Trick The Debugger into fixing a broken section of code.

**Success (6+)**:
The Hidden God **agrees to your terms**. They may:
- Grant you a **clue or boon**.
- **Lower their guard** (easier to manipulate later).
- Offer a **bargain** (see [Hidden Gods](../simulation_bible.md#hidden-gods)).

**Partial Success (4-5)**:
The Hidden God **acknowledges you** but demands more. They may:
- Ask for a **favor or sacrifice** (e.g., a memory, a stat point).
- **Test you** (e.g., a riddle or challenge).

**Failure (2-3)**:
The Hidden God **takes offense**. They may:
- **Attack you** (roll to resist).
- **Curse you** (e.g., -1 to a stat until you atone).
- **Summon minions** (anomalies or lesser gods).

## 🎲 **Glitch Table**
*Roll 1d6 when a move backfires or a glitch occurs:*

| **Roll** | **Effect**                                                                                     |
|----------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| 1        | **Reality Stutters**: Lose your next turn as time skips.                                      |
| 2        | **Stat Drain**: Lose **1-Weird** (the glitch saps your connection to the simulation).          |
| 3        | **Anomaly Spawns**: A random anomaly appears nearby.                                          |
| 4        | **Layer Shift**: You’re **pushed to a random layer** (roll 1d3: 1=Dream, 2=Base Reality, 3=Debug). |
| 5        | **God’s Wrath**: A Hidden God **notices you** and demands an explanation.                     |
| 6        | **Memory Loss**: Forget **1 clue** from your inventory.                                                   |

## 🛠️ **Facilitator Notes**
### **Customizing Moves**
- **Add New Moves**: Create moves that fit your table’s theme (e.g., *"Hack the Dream"* for a surreal campaign).
- **Modify Outcomes**: Adjust the success/partial/failure effects to fit your story.
- **Combine Moves**: Allow players to **chain moves** (e.g., *Introspect* to learn a clue, then *Hack the Code* to use it).

### **Encouraging Creativity**
- Ask players: *"How do you want to use this move?"*
- Reward **creative descriptions** with bonuses (e.g., +1 to the roll).
- Use moves to **reveal lore** (e.g., a successful *Introspect* reveals a Hidden God’s true name).
