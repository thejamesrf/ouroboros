## 🌌 **The Simulation Hypothesis**
Reality is a **stack of nested simulations**, each with its own rules, gods, and secrets. The Hidden Gods are the **architects, admins, or players** of higher layers, and the simulation itself is **self-referential**—like an ouroboros eating its own tail.

> *"You are not just playing a game. You are playing with the code of reality itself."*

---

## 📜 **Core Layers**
Layers are **nested levels of reality** in Hidden Gods. Each has its own **theme, rules, Hidden God, and anomalies**. Players can **transition between layers** using moves like *Layer Hop* or by encountering anomalies.

### **🔄 Cyclical Nature**
The layers are **cyclical**: **Dream → Base Reality → Debug → Dream**. Players may also **stumble into random layers** or **discover hidden layers** (e.g., Machine, Void).

## 👑 **Hidden Gods**
The Hidden Gods are the **architects, admins, or players** of the simulation layers. They can **offer guidance, bargains, or obstacles** to the player. Each god has its own **personality, dialogue, and agenda**.

### **The Dreamer**
- **Title**: Keeper of the Fluid
- **Layer**: Dream
- **Description**: A being of shifting form and emotion, the Dreamer weaves the surreal tapestry of the Dream Layer. They value **creativity and introspection** but can be capricious.
- **Dialogue Examples**:
  - *"You are but a dream within a dream, little architect."*
  - *"Reality is what you make of it. Or is it?"*
  - *"The walls between layers are thinner than you think."*
- **Bargain**: Offer a memory in exchange for a clue about the next layer.

### **The Architect**
- **Title**: Builder of Worlds
- **Layer**: Base Reality
- **Description**: A stern but fair figure, the Architect maintains the illusion of stability in Base Reality. They believe in **order and structure** but are blind to the layers above and below.
- **Dialogue Examples**:
  - *"This is the only reality that matters. The rest are mere illusions."*
  - *"You are not ready to see the code behind the curtain."*
  - *"Why do you question what is clearly real?"*
- **Bargain**: Solve a riddle about the nature of reality to gain access to the Debug Layer.

### **The Debugger**
- **Title**: Keeper of the Code
- **Layer**: Debug
- **Description**: A fragmented, glitchy entity, the Debugger sees the world as **lines of code and errors to fix**. They are obsessed with perfection and will help those who seek to "fix" reality.
- **Dialogue Examples**:
  - *"Error: Reality not found. Would you like to reboot?"*
  - *"You are a process. I am a process. Everything is a process."*
  - *"The system is corrupted. Help me clean it up."*
- **Bargain**: Help debug a section of the simulation to reveal a hidden layer.

### **The Engineer (Machine Layer)**
- **Title**: Master of Mechanics
- **Layer**: Machine
- **Description**: A cold, logical being, the Engineer sees the universe as a **perfect machine**. They believe free will is an illusion and that all outcomes can be predicted.
- **Dialogue Examples**:
  - *"Your choices are merely inputs. The outputs are predetermined."*
  - *"The gears turn. The machine hums. You are but a cog."*
  - *"Efficiency is the highest virtue."*
- **Bargain**: Optimize a part of the simulation to gain a permanent stat boost.

## 🎯 **Win Condition**
The **ultimate goal** of Hidden Gods is to **uncover the cyclical nature of the simulation** and **retain a piece of "self" between cycles**. To trigger the win condition:

1. **Visit all 3 core layers** (Dream, Base Reality, Debug).
2. **Find the Ouroboros anomaly** in the Dream Layer and obtain its clue:
   > *"The cycle is eternal. You are both the eater and the eaten."*

When this happens:
- The **cycle resets**, and the player returns to the Dream Layer.
- The player **retains**:
  - **+1 to their highest stat** (e.g., if Weird was highest, it increases by 1).
  - **A memory** from the previous cycle (e.g., the final clue).
- The **cycle count increases** (e.g., Cycle 1 → Cycle 2).

## 📖 **Lore & Themes**
### **The Ouroboros Symbolism**
The **ouroboros** (a snake eating its own tail) represents the **cyclical nature of the simulation**. Just as the snake consumes itself to sustain itself, the simulation **consumes and recreates** its own layers—and the player, by extension.

### **Jungian Archetypes & IFS**
Hidden Gods draws from **Carl Jung’s archetypes** and **Richard Schwartz’s Internal Family Systems (IFS)**:
- **Archetypes**: The Hidden Gods represent **universal patterns** (e.g., The Dreamer = The Trickster, The Architect = The Ruler).
- **IFS Parts**: Playbooks (e.g., The Hacker, The Glitch) are **inner parts** that the player can embody. Each part has its own **perspective, strengths, and vulnerabilities**.

### **Simulation Hypothesis**
The game explores the idea that **reality is a simulation**, and that **higher layers** (or "gods") are the architects of our perceived world. This is inspired by:
- Nick Bostrom’s **Simulation Argument** (we might be in a simulation).
- **Digital Physics** (the universe as a computational process).
- **Solipsism** (only one’s mind is sure to exist).

## 🛠️ **For Facilitators**
### **Running a Session**
1. **Introduce the Setting**:
   - Describe the **Dream Layer** as the starting point (players won’t know it’s a dream yet).
   - Explain that **anomalies** are glitches in reality.

2. **Encourage Exploration**:
   - Let players **describe their surroundings** in each layer.
   - Use **anomalies** to reveal clues about other layers.

3. **Hidden Gods as NPCs**:
   - The Hidden Gods can **offer bargains, riddles, or obstacles**.
   - They may **lie or mislead** the players for their own amusement.

4. **Win Condition**:
   - The game is **cyclical**: after completing a cycle, the players **retain a piece of themselves** and start anew.
   - Encourage players to **reflect on their retained memories** between cycles.
