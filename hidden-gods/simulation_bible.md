# Hidden Gods — Simulation Bible

> 🟢 *The shared world-building bible for Hidden Gods: layers, gods, anomalies, and the rules that hold the simulation together (until they don't).*

---

## 🌌 **Purpose**
This document is the **canonical lore reference** for Hidden Gods. Players and Facilitators co-create it as they play. Everything here is **negotiable at the table**—treat it as a living text, not a fixed rulebook.

> *"The simulation has a bible. The fun part is that the players get to rewrite it."*

---

## 🪐 **Simulation Layers**
Each layer is a **nested stratum of reality** with its own theme, rules, gods, and anomalies.

| Layer          | Theme                          | Rules                                                                 | Gods               |
|----------------|--------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------|--------------------|
| **Base Reality** | "Normal" life                 | Standard physics, but with subtle glitches.                          | The Architect      |
| **Debug**       | Glitchy, monochrome           | Code is visible as geometry; time is non-linear.                     | The Debugger       |
| **Dream**       | Surreal, emotional             | Rules are fluid; emotions shape reality.                            | The Dreamer        |
| **Machine**     | Mechanical, cold              | Everything is a machine; free will is an illusion.                   | The Engineer       |

> *Add new layers here as your table discovers them.*

---

## 🕯️ **The Hidden Gods**
The architects, admins, and players of higher simulation layers. They are **not omniscient**—each is blind to the layers above their own.

## 🔍 **Anomalies**
Glitches, clues, and disruptions that hint at higher or lower layers.

### **Example: The Echoing Door**
- **Manifestation**: A door that repeats the last 3 seconds of sound when opened.
- **Clue**: "The air smells like ozone."
- **Purpose**: To test the party's perception of time.
- **Risk**: Roll+Weird to resist disorientation (2-Weird).

> *Catalog new anomalies here as your table encounters them.*
