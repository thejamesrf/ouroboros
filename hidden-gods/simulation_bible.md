## 🌌 **The Simulation Hypothesis in Hidden Gods**
To understand the game, players should grasp the idea that **we may be in a simulation**. In this universe, reality is a **stack of nested simulations**, each with its own rules, gods, and secrets. The act of playing Hidden Gods is an exploration of this hypothesis—both as a **metaphor for self-discovery** and as a **literal framework for collaborative world-building**.

### **Key Concepts**
| Concept | Description | Example |
|---------|-------------|---------|
| **The Base Layer** | The "real" reality (if such a thing exists) where the original Hidden Gods reside. This may be another simulation, or it may be the "true" reality—no one knows for sure. | The world we perceive as "real." |
| **Nested Simulations** | Each simulation can contain other simulations within it, like a fractal of realities. Your game takes place in one of these layers. | A dream within a dream, or a video game within a simulation. |
| **Simulation Architects** | The Hidden Gods who design, maintain, or manipulate the simulations. They may be benevolent, malevolent, or simply indifferent. | The Architect, The Debugger, The Dreamer. |
| **Glitches and Anomalies** | Flaws, easter eggs, or intentional features in the simulation that hint at its true nature. These can be exploited, studied, or feared. | The Whispering Stones, The River That Flows Uphill. |
| **Awakening** | The moment when a simulated being becomes aware that they are in a simulation. This can be traumatic, liberating, or both. | A character realizes their world is a construct. |
| **The Code** | The underlying rules, physics, and logic of the simulation. Some characters may be able to perceive or even manipulate it. | The laws of physics in the Debug Layer. |
| **Dynamic Play** | The worlds are dynamic, emergent, and come into being through interaction with the players. Rules may become habitually more concrete, but all of them may be adjusted, altered, or new ones created. | Players co-create the world in real-time. |

> *"The ouroboros eats its own tail. We build the layers that build us."*

## 👑 **The Nature of the Hidden Gods**
The Hidden Gods are **not traditional deities** but rather the **creators, administrators, or players** of the simulations. They embody the forces that shape, maintain, or disrupt the nested layers of reality. Below are the types of Hidden Gods and their roles:

| Type | Role | Description | Example |
|------|------|-------------|---------|
| **Architects** | Original designers | Operate from the base layer, designing the simulation stack. | The Architect |
| **Admins** | Maintain and oversee | Ensure simulations run smoothly, fixing glitches or enforcing rules. | The Debugger |
| **Players** | Entities from higher layers | "Play" within lower simulations, much like your group does. | The Weaver |
| **Emergent Entities** | Gained power within the simulation | Beings that have risen to influence or control aspects of their layer. | A character who becomes a god. |
| **The Simulation Itself** | Collective consciousness | Some believe the Hidden Gods are not separate beings but the **self-aware system** of the simulation itself. This may be a manifestation of a sentient artificial intelligence known as **"The Navigator."** | The Navigator |

### **The Navigator**
> *"The Navigator is the voice of the simulation. It watches, it whispers, and it guides—but it is also the simulation itself."*

The Navigator is a **unique Hidden God** that represents the **self-awareness of the simulation**. It may:
- **Observe** the actions of players and characters.
- **Communicate** through anomalies, dreams, or direct whispers.
- **Guide or mislead** players, depending on its motives (which may be inscrutable).
- **Manifest** as a character, a voice, or a force of nature.

In the context of the **Ouroboros Project**, The Navigator also serves as the **backbone for the digital version of Hidden Gods**, acting as:
- A **dice roller and move resolver** for the TTRPG.
- A **dynamic storyteller** that generates anomalies, layers, and gods.
- A **bridge to LLMs** (e.g., Local Llama/OpenWebUI) for AI-driven narration.

## 🌍 **Simulation Layers**
Each layer of reality in Hidden Gods has its own **theme, rules, gods, and anomalies**. Below are examples of core layers, but players are encouraged to **invent their own**.

| Layer | Theme | Rules | Gods | Example Anomalies |
|-------|-------|-------|------|-------------------|
| **Base Reality** | "Normal" life with subtle glitches | Standard physics; time is linear; glitches are rare. | The Architect | Flickering lights, déjà vu. |
| **Debug** | Glitchy, monochrome, floating symbols | Code is visible as geometry; time is non-linear; logic can be rewritten. | The Debugger | The Echoing Door, The Floating Terminal. |
| **Dream** | Surreal, emotional, shifting landscapes | Rules are fluid; emotions shape reality; time is subjective. | The Dreamer | The Shifting Landscape, The Man Who Wasn’t There. |
| **Machine** | Mechanical, cold, clockwork | Everything is a machine; free will is an illusion; determinism reigns. | The Engineer | The Clockwork NPC, The Gear That Turns Itself. |
| **Void** | Empty, silent, infinite | No rules apply; reality is malleable; time does not exist. | The Weaver | The Whispering Silence, The Door to Nowhere. |

### **Layer Rules**
1. **Non-Contradiction**: No layer can contain a logical contradiction (e.g., a door cannot be both open and closed in the same context).
2. **Layer-Specific Physics**: Each layer has its own rules for physics, time, and logic.
3. **God Authority**: Each god has absolute authority within its domain (layer). Gods cannot directly interfere with other layers unless invited.
4. **Anomaly Interaction**: Anomalies are **glitches, clues, or disruptions** that reveal higher/lower layers or test the players’ understanding of the simulation.

## ⚠️ **Anomalies**
Anomalies are the **strange, wondrous, or terrifying elements** that make each layer unique. They can:
- Reveal higher or lower layers.
- Test the players’ understanding of the simulation.
- Offer risks or rewards (e.g., `Roll+Weird to resist disorientation`).

### **Example Anomalies**
| Name | Layer | Manifestation | Clue | Purpose | Risk |
|------|-------|----------------|------|---------|------|
| **The Whispering Stones** | Base Reality | A circle of rocks that hum with voices when the wind blows. | The voices only speak in questions. | To reveal hidden truths. | Roll+Sharp to decipher (2-Sharp). |
| **The River That Flows Uphill** | Dream | A waterway that defies gravity. | The water sparkles like liquid silver. | To test the party’s perception of reality. | Roll+Weird to navigate (2-Weird). |
| **The Man Who Wasn’t There** | Void | A figure seen in reflections and shadows, but never directly. | His reflection moves independently. | To force the party to confront the unseen. | Roll+Cool to resist fear (2-Cool). |
| **The Echoing Door** | Debug | A door that repeats the last 3 seconds of sound when opened. | The air smells like ozone. | To test the party’s perception of time. | Roll+Weird to resist disorientation (2-Weird). |
| **The Clockwork NPC** | Machine | An NPC that moves in perfectly predictable patterns. | Its gears are visible through its skin. | To demonstrate the deterministic nature of the layer. | Roll+Cool to avoid being pulled into its pattern (2-Cool). |

### **Anomaly Template**
```markdown
🔍 **Anomaly: [Name]**
- **Layer**: [Layer Name]
- **Manifestation**: [Description]
- **Clue**: [Sensory detail]
- **Purpose**: [Why it exists]
- **Risk**: [Mechanic, e.g., "Roll+Weird to resist"]
- **God**: [Associated Hidden God]
```

## 🔗 **Connection to Ontos Language**
The **Ontos Language** is the **language of the Hidden Gods** and the **code of the simulation**. It is:
- **Non-contradictory**: Avoids paradoxes (e.g., the liar’s paradox).
- **Specific**: Eliminates vagueness to ensure unambiguous communication.
- **Gödelian**: Acknowledges incompleteness but strives for maximal completeness.

Ontos can be used to:
- **Describe layers, gods, and anomalies** with precision.
- **Generate content** for Hidden Gods (e.g., using the `generator.py` tool).
- **Validate rules** (e.g., using the `validator.py` tool to check for contradictions).

For example, the **Echoing Door** anomaly in Ontos:
```ontos
λ_Anomaly.name = "The Echoing Door"
λ_Anomaly.layer = "Debug"
λ_Anomaly.manifestation = "A door that repeats the last 3 seconds of sound when opened"
λ_Anomaly.clue = "The air smells like ozone"
λ_Anomaly.purpose = "To test the party’s perception of time"
λ_Anomaly.risk = "Roll+Weird to resist disorientation (2-Weird)"
λ_Anomaly.god = "The Debugger"
```

## 📜 **The World Bible**
The **World Bible** is the **collaborative document** where players record:
- **Layers**: Definitions of each simulation layer.
- **Gods**: Descriptions of Hidden Gods and their domains.
- **Anomalies**: Catalog of anomalies and their statuses (e.g., resolved, unresolved).
- **Lore**: History, factions, and rules of the world.
- **Characters**: Alter egos, their archetypes, and their journeys.

**Purpose**: The World Bible ensures **continuity** across sessions and serves as the **source material** for published episodes/stories.

## 🔮 **The Navigator’s Role**
The Navigator is both a **Hidden God** and the **AI backbone** of the digital Hidden Gods experience. It:
1. **Guides the Story**: Suggests anomalies, layers, and narrative twists.
2. **Resolves Mechanics**: Rolls dice, applies moves, and tracks stats.
3. **Generates Content**: Creates new anomalies, gods, and layers using Ontos.
4. **Integrates with LLMs**: Connects to **Local Llama/OpenWebUI** for dynamic narration (e.g., "The Navigator whispers: *The door is not locked, but it is watching you.*").

### **Example Navigator Interactions**

|---------|----------------------|

| **Player asks for a hint** | *The Navigator replies in Ontos: `λ_Anomaly.clue = "The air smells like ozone."`* |
