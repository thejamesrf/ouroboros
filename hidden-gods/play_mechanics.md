## 🎲 The Dice

Hidden Gods uses the **Powered by the Apocalypse (PbtA) dice system**:
- Roll **2d6** (two six-sided dice) and add the relevant **stat** (e.g., +Weird, +Sharp).
- **6 or lower**: A **miss**—things go badly. The Facilitator makes a move.
- **7-9**: A **partial success**—you do it, but with a complication or cost.
- **10+**: A **full success**—you do it, and maybe get an additional benefit.

> *"The dice don’t decide your fate—they reveal the simulation’s response to your actions."*

## 🏃 Basic Moves

These are the **fundamental actions** characters can take in Hidden Gods. All players have access to these moves.

### Act Under Pressure
When you do something **risky or under stress**, **roll+Cool**.
- **10+**: You do it.
- **7-9**: You do it, but choose one:
  - It takes longer than expected.
  - You draw unwanted attention.
  - It costs you something (a resource, a relationship, etc.).
- **Miss**: The Facilitator makes a move.

**Example**: *You try to pick a lock while guards patrol nearby.*

---

### Go Aggro
When you **threaten or attack someone directly**, **roll+Hard**.
- **10+**: They have to choose:
  - Force your hand and suck it up.
  - Cave and do what you want.
- **7-9**: They can choose one of the above, **or** one of:
  - Get the hell out of your way.
  - Barricade themselves securely in.
  - Give you something they think you want.
  - Back off calmly, hands where you can see.
- **Miss**: Be prepared for the worst.

**Example**: *You draw your sword and demand the guard surrender.*

---

### Seduce or Manipulate
When you try to **influence someone through charm, persuasion, or deception**, **roll+Hot**.
- **10+**: They’ll go along with you unless something betrays your intent.
- **7-9**: They’ll go along but need some assurance first.
- **Miss**: Be prepared for the worst.

**Example**: *You try to convince the bartender to give you free information.*

---

### Read a Situation
When you **assess a scene or situation**, **roll+Sharp**.
- **10+**: Ask **3 questions** from the list below.
- **7-9**: Ask **1 question**.
- **Miss**: Ask 1 anyway, but be prepared for the worst.

**Questions**:
- What’s my best escape route/way in/way past?
- Which enemy is most vulnerable to me?
- Which enemy is the biggest threat?
- What should I be on the lookout for?
- What’s my enemy’s true position?
- Who’s in control here?

**Example**: *You scan a crowded marketplace for threats.*

---

### Read a Person
When you **study someone in a charged interaction**, **roll+Sharp**.
- **10+**: Hold **3**.
- **7-9**: Hold **1**.
- **Miss**: Ask 1 anyway, but be prepared for the worst.

**Spend your hold** to ask their player:
- Are you telling the truth?
- What are you really feeling?
- What do you intend to do?
- What do you wish I’d do?
- How could I get you to ___?

**Example**: *You lock eyes with a suspicious stranger and try to read their intentions.*

---

### Open Your Brain
When you **open your mind to the psychic maelstrom** (the hidden currents of reality, the collective unconscious, the voice of the Hidden Gods), **roll+Weird**.
- **10+**: The Facilitator tells you something **new and interesting with good detail**.
- **7-9**: You get an **impression**.
- **Miss**: Be prepared for the worst.

**Example**: *You meditate to seek guidance from the Hidden Gods.*

---

### Help or Interfere
When you **assist or hinder someone** making a roll, **roll+Hx** with them.
- **10+**: They get **+2** (help) or **-2** (interfere) to their roll.
- **7-9**: They get **+1** or **-1**.
- **Miss**: Be prepared for the worst.

**Example**: *You try to distract the guard while your ally picks the lock.*

## 🌪️ The Psychic Maelstrom

In Hidden Gods, the **psychic maelstrom** represents:
- The **collective unconscious** of the simulation.
- The **voice of the Hidden Gods**.
- The **underlying reality** beneath appearances.
- The **wellspring of creation and destruction**.

Characters can interact with it through:
- **Opening their brain** (the *Open Your Brain* move).
- **Special moves** that manipulate or interpret it (e.g., *World-Shaper*, *Reality Warp*).
- **Anomalies** that are physical manifestations of it (e.g., *The Whispering Stones*).

### **Effects of the Psychic Maelstrom**
When the psychic maelstrom is involved, **strange things happen**:
- Reality can **bend** (e.g., time loops, gravity reverses).
- **Visions appear** (e.g., glimpses of other layers, prophetic dreams).
- The **Hidden Gods whisper** (e.g., cryptic messages, warnings, or riddles).
- **Anomalies activate** (e.g., *The River That Flows Uphill* starts flowing downward).

> *"The maelstrom is the simulation’s bloodstream. To hack the code, you must swim in its currents."*

## 🎯 Special Moves

Special moves are **unique abilities** tied to your **archetype** or **custom choices**. Each character starts with **2 special moves** (from their archetype or the general list).

### **Example Special Moves**
| Move | Archetype | Description |
|------|-----------|-------------|
| **World-Shaper** | Creator | Describe a new aspect of the world, and it becomes real. Roll+Weird. |
| **Deep Insight** | Sage | Seek understanding of a person or situation. Roll+Sharp. |
| **Defy Authority** | Rebel | Challenge a rule or norm. Roll+Hot. |
| **Pathfinder** | Explorer | Navigate unfamiliar terrain. Roll+Sharp. |
| **Reality Warp** | Magician | Bend the rules of reality. Roll+Weird. |
| **Healing Touch** | Caregiver | Comfort or heal someone. Roll+Sharp. |

## 🔮 The Navigator’s Role in Play

The **Navigator** (both as a Hidden God and as the AI tool) can:
1. **Suggest Moves**: Recommend actions based on the current situation.
2. **Generate Anomalies**: Create new anomalies dynamically during play.
3. **Resolve Rolls**: Automate dice rolling and move outcomes.
4. **Narrate**: Provide **immersive descriptions** of events (e.g., "The Navigator’s voice echoes: *The door is not locked, but it is watching you.*").

### **Example Navigator Interactions**
| Player Action | Navigator Response |
|---------------|--------------------|
| *Player rolls "Open Your Brain"* | *"The code hums with a familiar rhythm. You recognize the pattern—it’s the same as the Echoing Door’s."* |
| *Player enters the Debug Layer* | *"The air smells like ozone. The Navigator’s symbol—a shifting gear—appears in your peripheral vision."* |
| *Player asks for a hint* | *"The anomaly’s clue is: *The air smells like ozone.* Roll+Weird to investigate."* |
