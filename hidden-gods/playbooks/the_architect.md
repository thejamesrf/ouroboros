## 🎭 **Archetype Overview**
**Role**: The Builder
**Core Stat**: +2 Sharp, +1 Cool, -1 Hot
**Moves**: Hack the Code, Layer Hop, Introspect
**Backstory**: You designed a layer so perfect that even you couldn’t escape it. Then you did.

## 🎯 **Playbook Moves**
### **1. Blueprint Vision** *(Roll+Sharp)*
*See the underlying structure of a layer or anomaly.*

**Example Uses**:
- **Reveal the hidden code** of a layer.
- **Understand the purpose** of an anomaly.
- **Predict how a Hidden God** will act based on their design.

**Success (6+)**:
You **see the blueprint**. Choose one:
- Learn the **true function** of an anomaly or layer.
- Gain **+1 forward** to your next *Hack the Code* or *Layer Hop* move.
- **Temporarily stabilize** a collapsing section of a layer.

**Partial Success (4-5)**:
You see **part of the blueprint**, but it’s incomplete.

**Failure (2-3)**:
The blueprint **overwhelms you**. Take **1-Sharp** as your mind struggles to process it.

---

### **2. Structural Integrity** *(Roll+Cool)*
*Temporarily reinforce a section of the simulation to prevent collapse or glitches.*

**Example Uses**:
- **Stabilize a crumbling** part of the Debug Layer.
- **Protect an ally** from a glitch.
- **Create a safe path** through a dangerous area.

**Success (6+)**:
The structure **holds firm** for the scene or until you leave the area.

**Partial Success (4-5)**:
The structure is **partially stabilized**, but:
- It **costs you 1-Cool** to maintain.
- The simulation **notices** (a Hidden God may investigate).

**Failure (2-3)**:
The structure **collapses worse than before**. Roll on the **Glitch Table**.

---

### **3. Admin Override** *(Roll+Weird, 1-Sharp)*
*Temporarily assume administrative control over a small part of the simulation.*

**Example Uses**:
- **Disable an anomaly** permanently (for the session).
- **Command a Hidden God’s minion** to do your bidding.
- **Rewrite a local rule** (e.g., "gravity is optional here").

**Success (6+)**:
The simulation **obeys your command** for a short time.

**Partial Success (4-5)**:
The override works, but:
- It **costs an additional 1-Sharp**.
- The simulation **resists** (a Hidden God may counter your command).

**Failure (2-3)**:
The override **fails catastrophically**. Roll on the **Glitch Table** and take **+1 harm**.

## 🌌 **Role in the Simulation**
The Architect is the **builder of worlds**—they see the simulation as a **grand design** to be perfected. They **respect the rules** but also **understand how to bend them**. Hidden Gods see them as **either a colleague or a rival**, depending on their own role.

### **Strengths**
- **Best at understanding** the simulation’s structure.
- **Excels in Base Reality** (where order is most valued).
- **Can stabilize chaotic** situations.

### **Weaknesses**
- **Lacks passion** (low Hot means they’re not driven by emotion).
- **Overconfident** in their designs (may underestimate glitches).
- **Hidden Gods challenge them** (they’re seen as upstarts).

## 🎭 **Example Session**
**Facilitator**: *"The Dream Layer is collapsing around you, the walls melting like wax."*

**Architect Player**: *"I use Blueprint Vision to understand the layer’s structure."*
- Rolls **2d6 + Sharp (2)** → **10 (Success!)**
- **Result**: The player sees the **underlying code** of the Dream Layer and realizes it’s **a loop of recurring memories**.

**Architect Player**: *"I use Structural Integrity to stabilize the collapsing walls."*
- Rolls **2d6 + Cool (1)** → **8 (Success!)**
- **Result**: The walls **solidify temporarily**, giving the party time to escape.

## 📖 **Lore**
### **Origin Story**
The Architect was once a **minor admin** in the Machine Layer, tasked with maintaining a single server. But when they **discovered a flaw in the layer’s design**—a way to **build new layers from scratch**—they ascended. Now, they **roam the simulation**, leaving behind **perfect but empty worlds**.

### **The Architect’s Mark**
Wherever the Architect goes, reality **becomes more ordered**:
- **Geometry becomes precise** (e.g., walls are exactly 90 degrees).
- **Time flows smoothly** (no stutters or loops).
- **NPCs act predictably** (they follow scripts).

But this **perfection is eerie**—like a world without a soul.

### **The Ultimate Goal**
The Architect seeks to **design the perfect layer**—one so flawless that even the Hidden Gods **cannot find a flaw in it**. Some say this layer **already exists**, and the Architect is now trapped there, endlessly refining it.
