## 🎭 **Archetype Overview**
**Role**: The Outcast
**Core Stat**: +1 Weird, +1 Hot, -1 Charm, +1 Sharp, -1 Cool
**Moves**: Glitch Out, Introspect, Negotiate with a God
**Backstory**: You were cast out of the light, but the dark is where the truth hides.

## 🎯 **Playbook Moves**
### **1. Shadow Step** *(Roll+Weird)*
*Move silently through the unseen parts of the simulation.*

**Example Uses**:
- **Sneak past** a Hidden God’s minions.
- **Hide in plain sight** by blending into the shadows.
- **Follow an anomaly** without being detected.

**Success (6+)**:
You **move unseen** and leave no trace.

**Partial Success (4-5)**:
You move unseen, but:
- You **leave a faint glitch** behind (e.g., a flicker of movement).
- You take **1-Cool** from the effort.

**Failure (2-3)**:
You **stumble into the light**. Roll on the **Glitch Table**.

---

### **2. Unseen Hand** *(Roll+Sharp)*
*Manipulate objects or people from the shadows.*

**Example Uses**:
- **Steal an item** without being noticed.
- **Move an obstacle** out of the way.
- **Plant a clue** for an ally to find.

**Success (6+)**:
Your influence is **undetectable**.

**Partial Success (4-5)**:
The action works, but:
- Someone **notices something is off** (but can’t pinpoint what).
- You take **1-Sharp** from the mental strain.

**Failure (2-3)**:
Your action **backfires**. Roll on the **Glitch Table**.

---

### **3. Whisper in the Dark** *(Roll+Charm)*
*Plant a thought or memory in someone’s mind.*

**Example Uses**:
- **Convince an NPC** to trust you (without speaking).
- **Make a Hidden God doubt** their own motives.
- **Give an ally a clue** without words.

**Success (6+)**:
The thought **takes root** and feels like their own.

**Partial Success (4-5)**:
The thought is **planted**, but:
- The target **senses something is wrong**.
- You take **1-Charm** from the effort.

**Failure (2-3)**:
The target **realizes you’re in their mind**. They **turn on you** (or a Hidden God notices).

## 🌌 **Role in the Simulation**
The Shadow is the **simulation’s secret**—the part that **lurks in the unseen**, the **unspoken**, the **forgotten**. They don’t just **hide from the light**; they **thrive in its absence**. Hidden Gods **fear or ignore** them, for shadows are hard to grasp.

### **Strengths**
- **Excels at stealth** and unseen manipulation.
- **Sees hidden truths** (high Weird and Sharp).
- **Hard to detect** in the Dream or Debug Layers.

### **Weaknesses**
- **Unsettling to others** (low Charm and Cool).
- **Vulnerable in Base Reality** (where rules are strict).
- **Hidden Gods distrust them** (they’re seen as omens).

## 🎭 **Example Session**
**Facilitator**: *"The Debugger’s minions patrol the hexagonal grid. How do you proceed?"*

**Shadow Player**: *"I use Shadow Step to move unseen to the terminal."*
- Rolls **2d6 + Weird (1)** → **7 (Success!)**
- **Result**: The player **slips through the shadows**, unseen by the minions.

**Shadow Player**: *"I use Unseen Hand to input a command into the terminal."*
- Rolls **2d6 + Sharp (1)** → **8 (Success!)**
- **Result**: The terminal **accepts the command** without alerting the minions.

## 📈 **Advancement**
When you **level up**, choose one:
1. **+1 Weird** (see deeper into the unseen).
2. **+1 Sharp** (notice more hidden details).
3. **Learn a new move** (e.g., *"Nightmare"*—plant a fear in a target’s mind).
4. **Gain a signature item** (e.g., a **Cloak of Shadows** that lets you reroll a failed Weird roll once per session).

## 📖 **Lore**
### **Origin Story**
The Shadow was once a **part of a greater whole**—a fragment of a god or a player who was **cast out** for knowing too much. Now, it **drifts between layers**, a whisper in the dark, a **memory half-remembered**.

### **The Shadow’s Mark**
Wherever the Shadow goes:
- **Light dims** in their presence.
- **People forget** they were there.
- **Glitches appear** in the corners of the eye.

### **The Ultimate Goal**
The Shadow seeks to **reclaim its place in the light**—or to **erase the light entirely** and rule the darkness.

## 🛠️ **Facilitator Tips**
- **Embrace Stealth**: Let the Shadow **succeed at unseen actions**—it’s their specialty.
- **Hidden Gods React**: The Architect **ignores** them; The Dreamer **whispers to** them; The Debugger **tries to debug** them.
- **Layer Advantages**: The Shadow gets **+1 to Weird rolls** in the Dream Layer.
