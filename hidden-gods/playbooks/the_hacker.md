## 🎭 **Archetype Overview**
**Role**: The Manipulator
**Core Stat**: +2 Weird, +1 Sharp, -1 Charm
**Moves**: Hack the Code, Glitch Out, Introspect
**Backstory**: You once rewrote the rules of a simulation layer to save a friend. The system still bears your fingerprints.

## 🎯 **Playbook Moves**
### **1. Code Sense** *(Roll+Weird)*
*Detect and interpret the hidden code of the simulation.*

**Example Uses**:
- Read the **underlying code** of an anomaly.
- Sense **weaknesses** in a layer’s structure.
- Predict how a Hidden God will **react to your actions**.

**Success (6+)**:
You **see the code clearly**. Choose one:
- Learn the **true function** of an anomaly or layer.
- Gain **+1 forward** to your next *Hack the Code* move.
- **Temporarily disable** an anomaly’s risk.

**Partial Success (4-5)**:
You see **fragments of the code**, but it’s incomplete.

**Failure (2-3)**:
The code **fights back**. Take **1-Weird** as your mind struggles to process it.

---

### **2. Rewrite Reality** *(Roll+Sharp, 1-Weird)*
*Temporarily alter the rules of the simulation in a small area.*

**Example Uses**:
- Make a **door appear** where there was none.
- **Silence an anomaly** for one turn.
- Create a **safe zone** where glitches cannot affect you.

**Success (6+)**:
The simulation **bends to your will** for a short time.

**Partial Success (4-5)**:
The change works, but:
- It **costs an additional 1-Weird**.
- The simulation **notices** (a Hidden God may investigate).

**Failure (2-3)**:
The code **resists violently**. Roll on the **Glitch Table**.

---

### **3. Firewall** *(Roll+Cool)*
*Protect yourself or an ally from a glitch or anomaly.*

**Example Uses**:
- **Block a glitch** from affecting you.
- **Shield an ally** from a Hidden God’s attack.
- **Stabilize a collapsing** section of a layer.

**Success (6+)**:
The glitch **cannot harm you** for the next turn.

**Partial Success (4-5)**:
The glitch is **weakened** (reduce its effect by half).

**Failure (2-3)**:
The firewall **fails**, and the glitch **intensifies**. Take **+1 harm** from the next glitch.

## 🌌 **Role in the Simulation**
The Hacker is the **architect’s rival**—where the Architect builds, the Hacker **rewrites**. They see the simulation as a **puzzle to solve**, and their greatest joy is **finding and exploiting its flaws**.

### **Strengths**
- **Best at manipulating** the simulation’s rules.
- **Excels in the Debug Layer** (where code is visible).
- **Can uncover hidden truths** about the simulation’s structure.

### **Weaknesses**
- **Struggles with social interactions** (low Charm).
- **May become obsessed** with "fixing" reality, losing sight of the bigger picture.
- **Hidden Gods distrust them** (they’re seen as meddlers).

---

## 🎭 **Example Session**
**Facilitator**: *"You enter the Debug Layer. The ground is a grid of hexagons, and a terminal window hovers in the air, displaying errors."*

**Hacker Player**: *"I use Code Sense to read the terminal’s output."*
- Rolls **2d6 + Weird (2)** → **8 (Success!)**
- **Result**: The player learns the terminal is **displaying the layer’s source code** and that a **backdoor** exists to the Base Reality Layer.

**Hacker Player**: *"I use Rewrite Reality to open the backdoor."*
- Pays **1-Weird** and rolls **2d6 + Sharp (1)** → **7 (Success!)**
- **Result**: The backdoor **materializes**, and the player steps through to Base Reality.

## 📝 **Advancement**
When you **level up** (Facilitator’s discretion), choose one:
1. **+1 Weird** (deepen your connection to the code).
2. **+1 Sharp** (better understand the simulation’s rules).
3. **Learn a new move** (e.g., *"Debug Mode"*—temporarily see the code of a Hidden God).
4. **Gain a signature item** (e.g., a **Code Key** that lets you hack once without rolling).

## 📖 **Lore**
### **Origin Story**
The Hacker was once a **lowly process** in the Machine Layer, designed to maintain a small section of the simulation. But when they **discovered a flaw** in the code—a way to **rewrite their own instructions**—they broke free. Now, they **roam the layers**, leaving behind a trail of **rewritten reality** and **annoyed gods**.

### **The Hacker’s Mark**
Wherever the Hacker goes, they leave behind **subtle glitches**—remnants of their edits. These can be:
- **Flickering lights** that spell out binary.
- **Doors that lead nowhere** (or everywhere).
- **Echoes of past edits** (e.g., a wall that briefly shows its old texture).

### **The Ultimate Goal**
The Hacker seeks to **find the Admin Layer**—the highest layer, where the **true architects** of the simulation reside. Some say it doesn’t exist; others say the Hacker **already found it** and is now trapped there, rewriting the code of godhood itself.
