## 🎭 **Archetype Overview**
**Role**: The Disruptor
**Core Stat**: +2 Hot, +1 Charm, -1 Cool
**Moves**: Glitch Out, Layer Hop, Negotiate with a God
**Backstory**: You don’t remember a time when you weren’t breaking things. The simulation fears you.

---

## 📊 **Stats**
| **Stat**  | **Value** | **Description**                                                                                     |
|----------|-----------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Weird    | +0        | You’re not **connected** to the simulation’s glitches—you *are* the glitch.          |
| Cool     | -1        | You **struggle to stay grounded**; chaos follows you.                                              |
| Sharp    | +0        | You **see the world differently**, but not always clearly.                                         |
| Hot      | +2        | Your **passion for disruption** is unmatched.                                                     |
| Charm    | +1        | You have a **strange charisma**—others are drawn to your unpredictability.                     |

## 🎯 **Playbook Moves**
### **1. Unstable Form** *(Roll+Hot)*
*Temporarily alter your physical form to adapt to a situation.*

**Example Uses**:
- **Squeeze through** a small gap by becoming liquid.
- **Grow extra limbs** to climb a wall.
- **Turn invisible** to avoid detection.

**Success (6+)**:
Your form **shifts as needed**. The change lasts until you **use this move again** or **fail a roll**.

**Partial Success (4-5)**:
Your form changes, but:
- It’s **uncontrollable** (Facilitator chooses the form).
- You take **1-Hot** from the effort.

**Failure (2-3)**:
Your form **glitches violently**. Roll on the **Glitch Table**.

---

### **2. Static Discharge** *(Roll+Weird)*
*Release a burst of glitch energy to disrupt the simulation.*

**Example Uses**:
- **Short-circuit an anomaly** to disable it.
- **Overload a Hidden God’s** connection to the layer.
- **Create a distraction** to escape.

**Success (6+)**:
The simulation **stutters** in a small radius. Choose one:
- **Disable an anomaly** for the scene.
- **Force a Hidden God** to lose their next action.
- **Create a temporary glitch** (e.g., a wall disappears).

**Partial Success (4-5)**:
The discharge works, but:
- It **affects you too** (take 1-Weird).
- The simulation **notices** (a Hidden God may appear).

**Failure (2-3)**:
The energy **backfires**. Roll on the **Glitch Table**.

---

### **3. Glitch Step** *(Roll+Charm)*
*Teleport a short distance by exploiting a glitch in space.*

**Example Uses**:
- **Escape a trap** by phasing through a wall.
- **Reach a distant** anomaly or clue.
- **Avoid an attack** from a Hidden God.

**Success (6+)**:
You **reappear where you intended**.

**Partial Success (4-5)**:
You teleport, but:
- You **end up in a random** nearby location.
- You take **1-Cool** from the disorientation.

**Failure (2-3)**:
You **get stuck in the glitch**. You’re **incapacitated** until someone helps you or you roll a **6+ on Hot** to break free.

## 🌌 **Role in the Simulation**
The Glitch is the **simulation’s mistake**—a **living error** that defies the rules. They don’t just **break reality**; they **are the break**. Hidden Gods see them as **either a nuisance or a tool**, depending on their mood.

### **Strengths**
- **Excels at disruption** (high Hot).
- **Hard to predict** (even Hidden Gods struggle to control them).
- **Can exploit glitches** that others cannot.

### **Weaknesses**
- **Unstable** (low Cool means they’re often in chaos).
- **Unreliable** (their powers can backfire).
- **Feared by NPCs** (people and gods avoid them).

## 🎭 **Example Session**
**Facilitator**: *"The Debugger corners you in a hexagonal grid. 'You do not belong here,' it hisses."*

**Glitch Player**: *"I use Unstable Form to turn into liquid and slip through the grid."*
- Rolls **2d6 + Hot (2)** → **9 (Success!)**
- **Result**: The player **melts into the floor** and reappears behind The Debugger.

**Glitch Player**: *"I use Static Discharge to overload its connection to the layer."*
- Rolls **2d6 + Weird (0)** → **5 (Partial Success)**
- **Result**: The Debugger **stutters**, but the player takes **1-Weird** from the feedback.

## 📈 **Advancement**
When you **level up**, choose one:
1. **+1 Hot** (fuel your disruptive power).
2. **+1 Charm** (better manipulate others).
3. **Learn a new move** (e.g., *"Corrupt"*—permanently alter a small part of the simulation).
4. **Gain a signature item** (e.g., a **Glitch Stone** that lets you reroll a failed Hot roll once per session).

## 📖 **Lore**
### **Origin Story**
The Glitch was **never meant to exist**. It was a **random error** in the simulation’s code—a **segmentation fault** that somehow gained sentience. Now, it **drifts between layers**, leaving behind a trail of **broken rules and confused gods**.

### **The Glitch’s Mark**
Wherever the Glitch goes, reality **warps** in their wake:
- **Objects flicker** in and out of existence.
- **Time stutters** (a second repeats, or skips entirely).
- **NPCs act strangely** (e.g., an NPC might say, "Didn’t I just talk to you?").

### **The Ultimate Goal**
The Glitch seeks to **find its origin**—the exact moment and place where it **first appeared**. Some say this will **free them**; others say it will **erase them** from existence.

## 🛠️ **Facilitator Tips**
- **Embrace Chaos**: Let the Glitch’s moves **have unpredictable effects**—it’s part of their charm.
- **Hidden Gods React**: The Architect **hates** them; The Debugger **studies** them; The Dreamer **plays with** them.
- **Layer Advantages**: The Glitch gets **+1 to Hot rolls** in the Dream Layer (where reality is fluid).
