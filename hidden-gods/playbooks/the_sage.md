## 🎭 **Archetype Overview**
**Role**: The Scholar
**Core Stat**: +2 Sharp, +1 Weird, -1 Hot, -1 Charm
**Moves**: Introspect, Hack the Code, Layer Hop
**Backstory**: You’ve read every book in the Dream Layer’s library. Some of them read you back.

## 🎯 **Playbook Moves**
### **1. Lore Mastery** *(Roll+Sharp)*
*Recall or discover a hidden truth about the simulation.*

**Example Uses**:
- **Remember a forgotten** piece of lore about a layer or god.
- **Decipher an ancient** text or symbol.
- **Predict the behavior** of a Hidden God based on historical patterns.

**Success (6+)**:
You **recall the information perfectly**. Choose one:
- Learn a **hidden truth** about the current layer or god.
- Gain **+1 forward** to your next *Introspect* or *Hack the Code* move.
- **Temporarily understand** a language or code you shouldn’t know.

**Partial Success (4-5)**:
You recall **part of the truth**, but it’s incomplete or misleading.

**Failure (2-3)**:
The knowledge **overwhelms you**. Take **1-Sharp** as your mind struggles to process it.

---

### **2. Pattern Recognition** *(Roll+Weird)*
*See the underlying patterns in the simulation’s design.*

**Example Uses**:
- **Predict the next anomaly** to appear.
- **Find a hidden path** in a layer’s structure.
- **Understand the rules** of a new layer instantly.

**Success (6+)**:
You **see the pattern clearly**. Choose one:
- **Avoid a trap** or anomaly before it triggers.
- **Find a shortcut** to your destination.
- **Gain a clue** about the simulation’s design.

**Partial Success (4-5)**:
You see **part of the pattern**, but it’s not enough to act on.

**Failure (2-3)**:
The pattern **misleads you**. You **fall into a trap** or trigger an anomaly.

---

### **3. Wisdom of the Ages** *(Roll+Cool)*
*Draw upon ancient knowledge to guide your actions.*

**Example Uses**:
- **Advise an ally** on the best course of action.
- **Recall a legend** about a Hidden God’s weakness.
- **Resist a mental attack** with sheer wisdom.

**Success (6+)**:
Your wisdom **guides you true**. Choose one:
- **Give an ally +1 forward** to their next roll.
- **Learn a Hidden God’s true name** (gaining power over them).
- **Resist a mental effect** (e.g., fear, confusion).

**Partial Success (4-5)**:
Your wisdom helps, but:
- The advice is **vague or cryptic**.
- You take **1-Cool** from the mental strain.

**Failure (2-3)**:
Your wisdom **fails you**. You **doubt your own knowledge** (1-Sharp).

## 🌌 **Role in the Simulation**
The Sage is the **simulation’s historian**—they **seek knowledge** above all else, and the simulation’s **secrets are their prize**. Hidden Gods **respect but fear** them, for knowledge is power.

### **Strengths**
- **Best at understanding** the simulation’s lore and rules.
- **Excels in the Dream Layer** (where knowledge is fluid).
- **Can uncover hidden truths** that others miss.

### **Weaknesses**
- **Lacks passion** (low Hot means they’re not driven by emotion).
- **Socially awkward** (low Charm means they struggle to connect).
- **Hidden Gods hide from them** (they’re seen as threats).

## 🎭 **Example Session**
**Facilitator**: *"The terminal displays an ancient script. What do you do?"*

**Sage Player**: *"I use Lore Mastery to decipher it."*
- Rolls **2d6 + Sharp (2)** → **10 (Success!)**
- **Result**: The player **translates the script** and learns it’s a **warning from a past cycle**: *"Beware the Architect’s lies."*

**Sage Player**: *"I use Pattern Recognition to predict the next anomaly."*
- Rolls **2d6 + Weird (1)** → **8 (Success!)**
- **Result**: The player **sees the pattern** and realizes a **Floating Door** will appear in 3 turns.

## 📈 **Advancement**
When you **level up**, choose one:
1. **+1 Sharp** (deepen your understanding).
2. **+1 Weird** (see more hidden patterns).
3. **Learn a new move** (e.g., *"Omniscience"*—ask the Facilitator one yes/no question per session).
4. **Gain a signature item** (e.g., a **Tome of Secrets** that lets you reroll a failed Sharp roll once per session).

## 📖 **Lore**
### **Origin Story**
The Sage was once a **librarian in the Admin Layer**, tasked with **recording the history of the simulation**. But when they **discovered a forbidden truth**—that the simulation was **flawed by design**—they were **cast out**. Now, they **roam the layers**, gathering knowledge to **expose the truth**.

### **The Sage’s Mark**
Wherever the Sage goes:
- **Books appear** in unexpected places.
- **NPCs whisper secrets** they shouldn’t know.
- **Hidden symbols** reveal themselves.

### **The Ultimate Goal**
The Sage seeks to **uncover the Admin Layer’s location** and **read the simulation’s source code**—to **understand the mind of god**.

## 🛠️ **Facilitator Tips**
- **Reward Knowledge**: If the Sage **recalls a relevant lore detail**, give them **+1 forward** to their next roll.
- **Hidden Gods React**: The Architect **hides from** them; The Dreamer **tests** them; The Debugger **respects** them.
- **Layer Advantages**: The Sage gets **+1 to Sharp rolls** in the Dream Layer.
