## Conflict Resolution Modules

Hidden Gods includes optional modules for resolving conflict through dialogue and introspection.

---

## The Circle Method

When conflict arises, players can choose to enter a Circle for structured dialogue:

1. **State the Issue**: Clearly define what is in conflict.
2. **Speak from the Heart**: Each person shares their perspective without interruption.
3. **Listen Deeply**: Others listen without responding or judging.
4. **Reflect**: After everyone has spoken, each person reflects on what they have heard.
5. **Find Resolution**: Work together to find a path forward.

---

## Bohmian Dialogue

For deeper philosophical conflicts, use Bohmian Dialogue:

1. **Suspend Assumptions**: Everyone agrees to set aside their beliefs temporarily.
2. **Speak from Experience**: Share personal experiences, not abstract ideas.
3. **Explore Meaning**: Ask, "What does this mean to you?" rather than debating facts.
4. **Listen for Understanding**: The goal is to understand, not to agree.
5. **Emergent Insight**: Allow new understanding to emerge from the dialogue.

## Introspection Moves

### Inner Dialogue
When you have a moment of quiet, you can engage in inner dialogue between your parts. **Roll+Weird**.

- **10+**: You gain insight into a conflict between your parts and can choose to resolve it or let it continue.
- **7-9**: You gain insight but must act on it immediately.
- **Miss**: The conflict between your parts intensifies.

---

### Shadow Work
When you confront a difficult truth about yourself, **roll+Sharp**.

- **10+**: You integrate this truth and gain +1 forward related to it.
- **7-9**: You acknowledge the truth but struggle to accept it.
- **Miss**: You resist the truth and suffer -1 forward related to it.

---

### Archetype Reflection
When you reflect on how your archetype is manifesting, **roll+Cool**.

- **10+**: You understand your archetype’s influence and can choose to embrace or challenge it.
- **7-9**: You see your archetype’s influence but cannot change it yet.
- **Miss**: Your archetype controls you in an unexpected way.
